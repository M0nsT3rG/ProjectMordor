## Known limitations

- **No game integration exists, and none is permitted yet** (gate G0).
- The prototype is IPv4-only, unauthenticated and unencrypted. It is meant for loopback and LAN only.
- The LAN test is NOT_RUN.
- No peer authentication yet (D-013). GNS encrypts but accepts unauthenticated peers (KI-21).

## Milestones

- **Latest verified milestone:** real-time soak. The 3-process prototype passes fixed gates on lan, moderate and harsh profiles over 120 s each, including recovery from a 5 s client freeze (EXP-010). Also 48 simulated-clock tests.
- **Next planned milestone:** Phase 2A. That means recording D-007 and D-008, running a LAN test (with firewall approval), and hardening the prototype (timer fix, desync digests, delta snapshots).

## Feasibility assessment

**Unknown, not disproven.**

- Game integration (M1–M10) is a conditional NO-GO on the Steam build: the protection layout plus Denuvo, the EULA, and the project's anti-circumvention rule all block it.
- The game-independent network layer works.
